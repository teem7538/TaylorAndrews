Security & Privacy Policy for Taylor Andrews Campaign
Effective Date: 10/06/2026
END Date: 02/01/2026

1. Introduction
Taylor Andrews Campaign is committed to safeguarding the personal information, voting preferences, and financial data of our supporters. This Security Policy outlines the technical and organizational measures we employ to protect our website, donor database, and volunteer network against unauthorized access, data breaches, and cyber threats.

2. Scope
This policy applies to:

The public-facing website https://teem7538.github.io/
Backend systems (CRM, email marketing platforms, donation processors).
All volunteers, staff, and third-party contractors with access to campaign data.

3. Data We Collect & Protect
We categorize data into three tiers for security handling:

Tier 1 - Public Data: Names, public contact info, and voluntary comments.
Tier 2 - Personal Data (PII): Email addresses, phone numbers, mailing addresses, and demographic info.
Tier 3 - Sensitive Data: Payment information (credit cards), bank details, and internal strategy documents.

4. Technical Security Measures

A. Encryption & Transmission
HTTPS/TLS: All traffic to and from our website is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
Payment Encryption: All donation forms are processed via PCI-DSS compliant third-party gateways (e.g., ActBlue, WinRed, Stripe). We never store full credit card numbers on our own servers.

B. Access Control
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Mandatory for all staff, volunteers, and contractors accessing the CMS, email lists, or donor databases.
Principle of Least Privilege: Volunteers only receive access to the specific tools necessary for their role (e.g., phone bankers do not have access to donor financial data).
Strong Password Policy: All accounts must use complex passwords and are rotated every 90 days.

C. Infrastructure Security
Web Application Firewall (WAF): We use a WAF to filter and monitor HTTP traffic, blocking common attacks like SQL Injection and Cross-Site Scripting (XSS).
DDoS Protection: Our hosting provider utilizes cloud-based DDoS mitigation to ensure site availability during high-traffic events.
Regular Backups: Automated daily backups of all databases are stored in a separate, encrypted, off-site location.

5. Volunteer & Third-Party Security
Background Checks: Staff with access to Tier 3 (Sensitive) data undergo background checks in accordance with local election laws.
Training: All volunteers must complete a brief cybersecurity training module covering phishing identification and data handling before gaining access.
NDAs: All third-party vendors (digital agencies, consultancies) must sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

7. Incident Response Plan
In the event of a security breach or suspected data leak:

Containment: Immediately isolate affected systems and revoke compromised credentials.
Assessment: Determine the scope, type of data exposed, and number of individuals affected.

Notification:
Notify the Campaign Manager and Legal Counsel within 1 hour.
Notify affected individuals and relevant election authorities within 72 hours, as required by law (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, or state-specific laws).
Remediation: Patch vulnerabilities, reset passwords, and restore systems from clean backups.
Post-Mortem: Conduct a review to prevent recurrence.

7. Voter Data & Compliance
Election Law Compliance: We adhere to all local, state, and federal regulations regarding voter data usage (e.g., FEC rules in the US).
No Data Selling: We strictly prohibit the sale or rental of donor or voter data to third parties.
Data Retention: Unsubscribed supporter data is archived and deleted after 1 years to minimize risk exposure.

9. User Rights & Responsibilities
Opt-Out: Users may unsubscribe from emails or request deletion of their personal data by contacting privicy@TaylorAndrews.com
Reporting Vulnerabilities: If you discover a security vulnerability, please do not exploit it. Report it responsibly to security@TaylorAndrews.com or via our responsible disclosure page.
